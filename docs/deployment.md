# Deployment

## Mooring

- Attach the buoy to the mooring line with the two 316 stainless shackles.
- Keep the pressure port at least 300 mm below the surface at low tide.
- Record the unit id and the calibration row before releasing the buoy.

## Recovery

- Wake the logger with the magnet near the reed switch.
- Download the SD card before removing the battery.
- Rinse the pressure port with fresh water and store the kit dry.
